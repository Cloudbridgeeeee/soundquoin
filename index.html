How a SoundQuoin study is set up

  1. Upload the consented recordings

    Add audio or video files with the consent record for each participant.

  2. Read the transcripts and themes

    Transcripts arrive within hours, and themes follow once every session is in.

  3. Review and report

    Researchers approve or merge themes, then export the report for the client.

Questions

What research teams ask before a demo.

Ask about SoundQuoin

Which recordings can we upload?

Only interviews your participants agreed to have recorded. Each upload needs a consent record.

Does it write the findings for us?

No. It suggests themes and drafts summaries; researchers approve every theme before a report.

What does it cost?

Priced by hours of audio processed plus a team subscription. We quote after the demo.

Which languages does it handle?

English and Nigerian Pidgin today, including sessions that mix the two.

Who can see the transcripts?

Only the people you add to each study. Agencies can share a study with one client.
