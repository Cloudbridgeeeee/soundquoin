# Product

SoundQuoin: Turn conversations into research intelligence.

AI research analysis software that transcribes authorized interviews and identifies recurring themes across customer research sessions.

- Founder and CEO: David Olatunji
- Co-founder and CTO: Adaobi Okonkwo
- Industry: Market Research, Media Technology, NLP
- Customers: Research agencies, product research teams and consulting firms
- Core features: Transcription, topic extraction, interview summaries, semantic search, research reports
- MVP: Interview uploads and researcher-reviewed thematic analysis
- Planned AWS: Transcribe, Bedrock, S3, OpenSearch, Lambda
- Business model: Audio processing and team subscriptions
